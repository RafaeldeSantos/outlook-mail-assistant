Kapitel 22. Scorecards.

Scorecards messen Handlungen, Responsivität, Gegenseitigkeit und Routenqualität. Sie messen nicht den eigenen Wert.

Night scorecard.

Warm-up. 0: None. 1: Partial. 2: Completed. Target: 2.

Top target approached. 0: No. 1: Late. 2: Within 5 sec. Target: 2.

Responsive conversation. 0: Script/interview. 1: Mixed. 2: Follow-up + callback + relate. Target: 2.

Warmth oder challenge. 0: Cold or approval seeking. 1: Mixed. 2: Warm edge calibrated. Target: 2.

Position changes. 0: 0. 1: 1. 2: 2+. Target: 2.

Reciprocity gates. 0: Guessed. 1: One signal. 2: Two current signals. Target: 2.

Kiss window. 0: Avoided. 1: Detected. 2: Acted/slowed correctly. Target: 2.

Route quality. 0: Complex. 1: Acceptable. 2: Shortest transparent route. Target: 2.

Tier-2 close. 0: Missed strong logistics block. 1: Vague. 2: Concrete day/time. Target: As needed.

Consent oder exit. 0: Poor. 1: Mixed. 2: Clear and responsive. Target: 2.
