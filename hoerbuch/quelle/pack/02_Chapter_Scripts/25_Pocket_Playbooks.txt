Kapitel 25. Pocket Playbooks.

Die Pocket Playbooks verdichten das System auf wenige Regeln, die im Club tatsächlich abrufbar bleiben.

Core - 12 rules.

Erstens, Build the setup. Zweitens, Move within five seconds. Drittens, Use BISS, not a complex routine. Viertens, Ask a real follow-up when her answer has energy. Fünftens, Use callbacks and warm teasing. Sechstens, Status is discovered. Siebtens, Move early. Achtens, Require two current green signals before larger steps. Neuntens, Same-Night is primary, not compulsory. Zehntens, Use Tier 2 for strong chemistry plus hard logistics. Elftens, Direct invitation before playful frame switch. Zwölftens, No means exit, not a new routine.

Frankfurt.

Hotel guest: hook dann friend bridge dann outside dann kiss dann hotel bar/water.

Office out. Auto is a tool, not a destination.

Conversation.

BISS: Beobachtung dann Interpretation dann Spiel dann Schritt.

Loop: Acknowledge dann genuine follow-up dann comment/play dann relate dann move.

International simple English.

You caught my eye. I had to come say hi. dann What do you enjoy most about it? dann You look much more sensible than you probably are. dann Come outside with me for five minutes. dann I would like to kiss you. dann One last drink at the hotel bar?

Final operating rule.

Be selective about the woman, aggressive about social action, responsive in conversation, calm about the result, precise about logistics and absolute about consent.

Die Kernaussage: Selektiv bei der Frau, aggressiv bei der sozialen Handlung, ruhig beim Ergebnis und absolut bei Consent.
