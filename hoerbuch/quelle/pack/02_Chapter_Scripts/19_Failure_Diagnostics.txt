Kapitel 19. Failure Diagnostics.

Fehlerdiagnose heißt: nicht die ganze Persönlichkeit infrage stellen. Ihr lokalisiert die konkrete Stufe, an der die Interaktion abgebrochen ist.

Topfrau-Panik. Likely cause: Optik wird mit Bedeutung verwechselt.. Correction: „Optisch außergewöhnlich. Mehr weiß ich nicht.“ Move in five seconds..

Conversation feels scripted. Likely cause: Cold reads and lines are used instead of responding.. Correction: Ask one genuine follow-up, then callback to her actual detail..

Too cold. Likely cause: Challenge, mystery or silence used before a hook.. Correction: More warmth; challenge only after positive investment..

Friendly but flat. Likely cause: No standards, tension or movement.. Correction: Specific compliment, warm tease, eye contact and small position change..

No kiss window. Likely cause: Waiting for a perfect invitation.. Correction: Check two green signals, slow down and state intent..

Transition rejected. Likely cause: One signal was mistaken for reciprocity or route had too many steps.. Correction: Use two-current-signal gate and one short route..

Strong vibe discarded. Likely cause: Hard logistics treated as failure because Same-Night was impossible.. Correction: Concrete Tier-2 close with day and time..

Car becomes a dead end. Likely cause: Waiting in a closed space for her to invite you.. Correction: Short parked moment, direct optional invite once, then goodnight..

Follow-up dies. Likely cause: Artificial silence or vague reactivation.. Correction: Low frequency, high clarity and concrete next visit..

Alcohol risk. Likely cause: Time, water or subjective feeling used as proof.. Correction: Do not drive with any doubt; taxi fallback..

Post-night diagnostic.

Erstens, Did I approach the best woman I saw?. Zweitens, Did I respond to her actual details?. Drittens, Did I create warmth before challenge?. Viertens, Which two signals justified my largest move?. Fünftens, Did I use the shortest route?. Sechstens, If logistics blocked Same-Night, did I offer a concrete Tier-2 plan?. Siebtens, What one behavior should be removed next week?.

Die Kernaussage: Korrigiert immer nur den nächsten Engpass.
