Kapitel 28. Drill-Karten.

Drills isolieren jeweils ein Verhalten. Eine Fähigkeit wird erst dann zuverlässig, wenn sie unter realen Bedingungen oft genug wiederholt wurde.

Drill 1. Drill 01 - Passing Compliments.

Deliver ten specific compliments while continuing to move unless she stops you. Train low-need delivery.

Success = clear voice, no hovering, no demand for reaction.

Drill 2. Drill 02 - Five-Second Movement.

Every time you identify a top target, start walking before five seconds.

Success = zero repeated laps.

Drill 3. Drill 03 - BISS Openers.

Ten openers using observation dann interpretation dann play dann step. No complex routine.

Success: opener sounds situational and the second sentence follows her real reaction.

Drill 4. Drill 04 - Responsive Loop.

Acknowledge one detail, ask one real follow-up, comment, reveal one short related detail, then move.

Success: she feels heard and the interaction does not become an interview.

Drill 5. Drill 05 - Callback Precision.

Create ten callbacks from details she actually gave you; use cold reads only as light hypotheses.

Success: humor is specific to the interaction, not interchangeable script.

Drill 6. Drill 06 - Group Inclusion.

Open five groups and address every person before selecting the target.

Success = friend names and one group laugh.

Drill 7. Drill 07 - Clean Exit.

Leave five weak interactions within two minutes without apology or number close.

Success = positive emotional state after exit.

Drill 8. Drill 08 - Position Change.

Propose three small moves: other side of bar, outside, drink station.

Success = ask early, not after 30 minutes.

Drill 9. Drill 09 - Direct Kiss Intent.

Role-play and then use calm verbal intent when the window is real.

Success = no ambiguous lunging; graceful no.

Drill 10. Drill 10 - Friend Bridge.

Practice one sentence explaining the mini-plan to friends.

Success = transparent and relaxed.

Drill 11. Drill 11 - Logistics Scan.

Within ten minutes, identify exits, outdoor area, taxis, hotel direction and coat-check friction.

Success = plan remembered without phone.

Drill 12. Drill 12 - English Simplicity.

Use only sentences under twelve words for one full set.

Success = slower speech and better eye contact.

Drill 13. Drill 13 - Status Understatement.

Answer profession in under fifteen seconds and ask/statement back.

Success = no car, salary or deal monologue.

Drill 14. Drill 14 - Alcohol-Free Flow.

After warm-up, switch to alkoholfrei and maintain social energy.

Success = state does not depend on another drink.

Drill 15. Drill 15 - Top-Target Focus.

Once a top set hooks, stop scanning the room for ten minutes.

Success = full presence.

Drill 16. Drill 16 - One Clear Transition.

Make at least one honest venue-change proposal per serious night.

Success = concrete place and easy no.

Drill 17. Drill 17 - Rejection Recovery.

Within three minutes of rejection, complete one neutral social interaction.

Success = no phone retreat.

Drill 18. Drill 18 - Reciprocity Gate.

Before isolation, kiss or private transition, name two current green signals. If they are absent, slow down.

Success: every larger move follows repeated mutual investment, not one ambiguous signal.

Die Kernaussage: Pro Abend ein Drill, nicht acht gleichzeitig.
