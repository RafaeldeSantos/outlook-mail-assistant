Kapitel 27. Englische Field Scripts.

Die englischen Field Scripts halten die Sprache bewusst einfach. Eine klare, ruhige Delivery schlägt komplexes Vokabular.

Script 1. English: Solo woman at hotel bar.

Der Einstieg: “You look interesting. I had to say hi.”

Die Interaktion: Ask where she is from; comment on travel energy; one qualifier; move seats or order together.

Der Übergang oder Close: “I am having one last drink. Join me.” SHORT SENTENCES - SLOW DELIVERY - CLEAR PLAN

Script 2. English: Two Scandinavian women.

Der Einstieg: “You two look very calm. I do not believe it.”

Die Interaktion: Simple teasing, include both, avoid talking over them, personal-space calibration.

Der Übergang oder Close: “Come outside for five minutes. It is too loud.” SHORT SENTENCES - SLOW DELIVERY - CLEAR PLAN

Script 3. English: British group.

Der Einstieg: “Which one of you is the bad influence?”

Die Interaktion: Fast banter, self-amusement, then slow down with target.

Der Übergang oder Close: “I am leaving soon. One last drink at the hotel bar.” SHORT SENTENCES - SLOW DELIVERY - CLEAR PLAN

Script 4. English: Spanish woman in premium lounge.

Der Einstieg: “You have a very confident look. What is the story?”

Die Interaktion: Warm tone, less verbal irony, respect group connection.

Der Übergang oder Close: “Let us stand outside for a minute.” SHORT SENTENCES - SLOW DELIVERY - CLEAR PLAN

Script 5. English: She asks what you do.

Der Einstieg: “I sell sunglasses in Rwanda. Tough industry.”

Die Interaktion: Smile and immediately make the joke clear: “Actually, I work in business and run companies. It sounds more exciting than it usually is. What do you enjoy most about your work?”

Der Übergang oder Close: “That does not fit my first impression of you at all.” JOKE - TRUTH - FOLLOW-UP

Script 6. English: She says you are arrogant.

Der Einstieg: “Only on good days.”

Die Interaktion: If serious: “What exactly feels arrogant?” Listen.

Der Übergang oder Close: Do not give a speech about confidence. SHORT SENTENCES - SLOW DELIVERY - CLEAR PLAN

Script 7. English: Strong eye contact.

Der Einstieg: “Do we know each other, or did you just fall in love?”

Die Interaktion: Smile and transition to simple normal conversation.

Der Übergang oder Close: “You were looking very intensely.” SHORT SENTENCES - SLOW DELIVERY - CLEAR PLAN

Script 8. English: Low state.

Der Einstieg: “Your outfit is very well put together.”

Die Interaktion: Five passing compliments, then one real set.

Der Übergang oder Close: Goal: voice and movement, not number. SHORT SENTENCES - SLOW DELIVERY - CLEAR PLAN

Script 9. English: Group leaving.

Der Einstieg: “Are you leaving already? I was just about to meet you.”

Die Interaktion: Ask destination; two-minute interaction; suggest one drink only if practical.

Der Übergang oder Close: “Come for one quick drink before you go.” SHORT SENTENCES - SLOW DELIVERY - CLEAR PLAN

Script 10. English: Friend blocker.

Der Einstieg: “Tell your friends where we are going. We are only outside for five minutes.”

Die Interaktion: Be transparent; do not argue with friend.

Der Übergang oder Close: Return or update as promised. SHORT SENTENCES - SLOW DELIVERY - CLEAR PLAN

Script 11. English: Kiss window.

Der Einstieg: “I would like to kiss you.”

Die Interaktion: Pause; let her answer and move.

Der Übergang oder Close: “No problem. We can slow down.” SHORT SENTENCES - SLOW DELIVERY - CLEAR PLAN

Script 12. English: Hotel transition.

Der Einstieg: “I can come up for one glass of water if you want. Ten minutes, relaxed.”

Die Interaktion: Direct and optional first. Only after a kiss or very clear mutual sexual chemistry add the playful role reversal.

Der Übergang oder Close: “One glass of water. Ten minutes. And behave.” If she hesitates: “No problem. Good night.” DIRECT FIRST - PLAYFUL AFTER CHEMISTRY

Script 13. English: She is too drunk.

Der Einstieg: “We are not doing anything tonight. Let us get you back to your friends.”

Die Interaktion: Water, friends, taxi, hotel staff.

Der Übergang oder Close: No sexual escalation. SHORT SENTENCES - SLOW DELIVERY - CLEAR PLAN

Script 14. English: Short answers.

Der Einstieg: “You are very efficient with words tonight.”

Die Interaktion: One genuine follow-up to her last real detail or one observation. If the energy stays low, exit.

Der Übergang oder Close: “No worries. Have a good night.” ONE FOLLOW-UP - ONE RE-HOOK - EXIT

Script 15. English: Strong investment.

Der Einstieg: “You are making it very easy to like you.”

Die Interaktion: Warmth back; position change; kiss window.

Der Übergang oder Close: Do not manufacture coldness. SHORT SENTENCES - SLOW DELIVERY - CLEAR PLAN

Script 16. English: Reconnect in Marbella.

Der Einstieg: “I am back in Marbella next Friday. One drink at nine?”

Die Interaktion: Low frequency, high clarity. No artificial silence and no weeks of background chat.

Der Übergang oder Close: If the response is vague twice, stop carrying the conversation. CONCRETE DATE - LIGHT CONTACT

Die Kernaussage: Einfaches Englisch soll ziehen, nicht beeindrucken.
