Kapitel 12. Escalation & Reciprocity Gates.

Fast progression based on repeated mutual investment, not a mechanical touch ladder or one ambiguous signal.

Abschnitt: Two-current-signal rule.

Gate: Before isolation. Possible green signals: She asks back, extends conversation, accepts a small position change, re-engages after distraction, includes you socially.. Move: Quieter position or five minutes outside.. Gate: Before kiss. Possible green signals: She remains close, holds eye contact, reciprocates touch, extends isolation, responds to clear tension.. Move: Slow down; state intent or ask when unclear.. Gate: Before hotel/home. Possible green signals: She actively continues, understands destination, agrees clearly, friend logistics are handled, decision-making is clear.. Move: One transparent private-location proposal..

Abschnitt: Progression.

Abschnitt: Traffic lights.

GREEN. Repeated investment, proximity, questions, return eye contact, voluntary following, clear yes.

AMBER. Mixed signals, freezing, repeated friend checks, hesitation. Stop moving forward; create space or ask.

RED. No, pulling away, fear, strong intoxication or inability to decide. End escalation.
