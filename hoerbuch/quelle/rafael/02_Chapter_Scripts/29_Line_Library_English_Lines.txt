Kapitel 24 E. Line Library: English Lines.

Diese Sammlung ist kein auswendig zu lernendes Drehbuch. Nutze die Formulierungen als flexible Bausteine und passe sie an den echten Moment an.

Kategorie: Opening.

Nächster Baustein. You look like you are either having the best night here or you are done with everyone.

Beispiel. You caught my eye. I had to come say hi.

Beispiel. You two look like trouble. Which one is the bad influence?

Beispiel. Are you leaving already? I was just about to meet you.

Nächster Baustein. You look interesting. I had to say hi.

Beispiel. Your style is very well put together. I like it.

Kategorie: Conversation.

Beispiel. That does not fit my first impression of you at all.

Beispiel. Creative style, analytical eyes. You are confusing me.

Nächster Baustein. What do you enjoy most about it?

Beispiel. What do people usually get wrong about you?

Beispiel. That makes sense. You definitely look like the type who ...

Kategorie: Attraction.

Beispiel. I am starting to like you. This is inconvenient.

Nächster Baustein. We would get along very well and argue all the time.

Beispiel. You are cool. That makes me slightly suspicious.

Beispiel. There is something about you that attracts me and unsettles me.

Beispiel. You look much more sensible than you probably are.

Kategorie: Qualifying.

Nächster Baustein. What do people usually get wrong about you?

Beispiel. What is the most spontaneous thing you did this year?

Kategorie: Transition.

Beispiel. I can come up for one glass of water if you want. Ten minutes, relaxed.

Beispiel. I can come up for one glass of water if you want. Ten minutes, relaxed, no pressure.

Nächster Baustein. I will walk you up, have some water and then disappear – unless you make that difficult.

Beispiel. One glass of water. Ten minutes. And behave.

Beispiel. Come outside with me for five minutes. It is too loud.

Beispiel. Tell your friends. We are just getting some air.

Nächster Baustein. My hotel is two minutes away. We can have one last drink there if you want.

Beispiel. My hotel bar is around the corner. Let us have one last drink there.

Beispiel. Come over there with me. I cannot hear you here.

Beispiel. Come with me for two minutes. I want to hear you properly.

Nächster Baustein. Tell your friends where we are going.

Kategorie: Escalation.

Beispiel. Is this okay?

Beispiel. I would like to kiss you.

Beispiel. You are making it very difficult not to kiss you.

Nächster Baustein. No problem. We can slow down.

Kategorie: Exit.

Beispiel. No worries. Have a good night.

Kategorie: Follow-up.

Beispiel. Good meeting you. Did you get home safely?

Beispiel. Good seeing you. Hope you got home safely.

Nächster Baustein. I am back next Friday. One drink at nine?

Beispiel. I am back in Ibiza next Friday. One drink at nine?

Beispiel. I am back in Marbella next Friday. Let us have a drink.
