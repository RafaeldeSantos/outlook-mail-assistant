Kapitel 20. Follow-up After the Night.

Low frequency, high clarity. No background-chat dependency and no staged disappearance.

Tier 1 – Same Night continued. One warm, respectful message. If the vibe was exceptional, make a concrete local plan within days; otherwise keep it light.

Tier 2 – strong chemistry, hard logistics. A number is not orbiting when the interaction was real and the follow-up is concrete. Set a day and time instead of weeks of texting.

Abschnitt: Message bank.

Rule. Real scarcity is a full life. Artificial silence is not a strategy. Communicate rarely because there is little useful to say – and clearly when there is.
