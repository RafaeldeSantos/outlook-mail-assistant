Kapitel 30. Quick Reference Cards.

Diese Kurzregeln sind für die letzten Minuten vor dem Ausgehen oder als Reset im Taxi gedacht.

Quick Card 1. One-Minute Pre-Game. Setup first: short route, clear transport, phone away, shoulders down, slow exhale, first micro-interaction before scanning.

Quick Card 2. Top Target Rule. She is visually exceptional. That is all you know. Move in five seconds, use the simplest opener and only aim for the hook.

Quick Card 3. Weak Set Rule. Two minutes dann one re-hook dann clean exit.

Quick Card 4. Conversation Rule. BISS: observation dann interpretation dann play dann step. Inside the loop: acknowledge dann real follow-up dann comment dann relate dann move.

Quick Card 5. Group Rule. Open everyone dann create one shared moment dann include the friend dann focus gradually dann isolate transparently.

Quick Card 6. Frankfurt Rule. Two routes only: hotel guest dann outside dann kiss dann hotel; local dann outside dann kiss dann transparent taxi/Porsche option dann direct invite or concrete close.

Quick Card 7. Travel Rule. Simple English dann early kiss possible dann friend check dann nearby hotel bar.

Quick Card 8. Consent Rule. Every step separate, reversible and sober enough for a clear decision.

Quick Card 9. Driving Rule. No escalation while driving; do not drive with any doubt about sobriety or alertness.

Quick Card 10. Follow-up Rule. Low frequency, high clarity: one warm message, genuine light contact only when natural, then one concrete proposal.
