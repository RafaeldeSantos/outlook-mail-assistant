Kapitel 10. Attraction Stack.

Visual value creates entry. Warmth, specificity, standards and movement create tension without scripted hostility.

Abschnitt: Warmth / challenge calibration.

Phase: Approach. Default: 60% warmth / 40% edge. Bedeutung: Clear interest, relaxed play.. Phase: After hook. Default: 70% warmth / 30% challenge. Bedeutung: Reward real investment; do not become artificially cold.. Phase: Low resonance. Default: No harder push. Bedeutung: One re-hook, then exit..

Abschnitt: High-conversion play bank.

Abschnitt: Status handling.

Do. Let style, venue, social ease and logistics imply status. Answer the profession briefly and truthfully. Use callbacks and attention as the main attraction signal.

Do not. Quote income, explain assets, compete with every man mentioned, use women as staged social proof or turn the interaction into a pitch deck.

Top-target frame. „Sie ist optisch außergewöhnlich. Mehr weiß ich noch nicht.“ Anerkennung ohne Podest und ohne künstliche innere Abwertung.
