Abschnitt: English conversation formula.

Observation dann simple question dann comment dann playful assumption dann move.

Beispiel: “You have a confident look. Where are you from?” “Stockholm.” “That makes sense. Very calm, slightly dangerous. Come over there, I cannot hear you.”

Abschnitt: Words to avoid.

Do not reach for complicated idioms, long explanations or pseudo-native slang. Your accent is not a problem. Hesitation and overcomplication are.
