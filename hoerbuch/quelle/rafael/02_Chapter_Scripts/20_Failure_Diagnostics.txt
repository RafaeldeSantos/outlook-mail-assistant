Kapitel 19. Failure Diagnostics.

Change the leaking stage, not the whole personality.

Leak: Topfrau-Panik. Wahrscheinliche Ursache: Optik wird mit Bedeutung verwechselt.. Korrektur: „Optisch außergewöhnlich. Mehr weiß ich nicht.“ Move in five seconds.. Leak: Conversation feels scripted. Wahrscheinliche Ursache: Cold reads and lines are used instead of responding.. Korrektur: Ask one genuine follow-up, then callback to her actual detail.. Leak: Too cold. Wahrscheinliche Ursache: Challenge, mystery or silence used before a hook.. Korrektur: More warmth; challenge only after positive investment.. Leak: Friendly but flat. Wahrscheinliche Ursache: No standards, tension or movement.. Korrektur: Specific compliment, warm tease, eye contact and small position change.. Leak: No kiss window. Wahrscheinliche Ursache: Waiting for a perfect invitation.. Korrektur: Check two green signals, slow down and state intent.. Leak: Transition rejected. Wahrscheinliche Ursache: One signal was mistaken for reciprocity or route had too many steps.. Korrektur: Use two-current-signal gate and one short route.. Leak: Strong vibe discarded. Wahrscheinliche Ursache: Hard logistics treated as failure because Same Night was impossible.. Korrektur: Concrete Tier-2 close with day and time.. Leak: Car becomes a dead end. Wahrscheinliche Ursache: Waiting in a closed space for her to invite you.. Korrektur: Short parked moment, direct optional invite once, then goodnight.. Leak: Follow-up dies. Wahrscheinliche Ursache: Artificial silence or vague reactivation.. Korrektur: Low frequency, high clarity and concrete next visit.. Leak: Alcohol risk. Wahrscheinliche Ursache: Time, water or subjective feeling used as proof.. Korrektur: Do not drive with any doubt; taxi fallback..

Abschnitt: Post-night diagnostic.

Erstens: Did I approach the best woman I saw? Zweitens: Did I respond to her actual details? Drittens: Did I create warmth before challenge? Viertens: Which two signals justified my largest move? Fünftens: Did I use the shortest route? Sechstens: If logistics blocked Same Night, did I offer a concrete Tier-2 plan? Siebtens: What one behavior should be removed next week?
