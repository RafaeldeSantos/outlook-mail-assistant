Kapitel 27, Teil 1. English Field Scripts, Teil 1.

Sprich die englischen Sätze langsam, klar und ohne komplizierte Wörter. Die Wirkung kommt aus Ruhe, Blickkontakt und einem einfachen Plan.

English field script 1. English: Solo woman at hotel bar.

Open.

“You look interesting. I had to say hi.”

Run the interaction.

Ask where she is from; comment on travel energy; one qualifier; move seats or order together.

Transition / close.

“I am having one last drink. Join me.”

English field script 2. English: Two Scandinavian women.

Open.

“You two look very calm. I do not believe it.”

Run the interaction.

Simple teasing, include both, avoid talking over them, personal-space calibration.

Transition / close.

“Come outside for five minutes. It is too loud.”

English field script 3. English: British group.

Open.

“Which one of you is the bad influence?”

Run the interaction.

Fast banter, self-amusement, then slow down with target.

Transition / close.

“I am leaving soon. One last drink at the hotel bar.”

English field script 4. English: Spanish woman in premium lounge.

Open.

“You have a very confident look. What is the story?”

Run the interaction.

Warm tone, less verbal irony, respect group connection.

Transition / close.

“Let us stand outside for a minute.”

English field script 5. English: She asks what you do.

Open.

“I sell sunglasses in Rwanda. Tough industry.”

Run the interaction.

Smile and immediately make the joke clear: “Actually, I work in M&A. It sounds more exciting than it usually is. What do you enjoy most about your work?”

Transition / close.

“That does not fit my first impression of you at all.”

English field script 6. English: She says you are arrogant.

Open.

“Only on good days.”

Run the interaction.

If serious: “What exactly feels arrogant?” Listen.

Transition / close.

Do not give a speech about confidence.

English field script 7. English: Strong eye contact.

Open.

“Do we know each other, or did you just fall in love?”

Run the interaction.

Smile and transition to simple normal conversation.

Transition / close.

“You were looking very intensely.”

English field script 8. English: Low state.

Open.

“Your outfit is very well put together.”

Run the interaction.

Five passing compliments, then one real set.

Transition / close.

Goal: voice and movement, not number.
