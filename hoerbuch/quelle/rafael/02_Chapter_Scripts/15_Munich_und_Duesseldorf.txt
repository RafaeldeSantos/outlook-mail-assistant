Kapitel 14. Munich & Duesseldorf.

Use more in-venue momentum than Frankfurt, while keeping the same selective archetype.

Munich. Erstens: Earlier social proof through table or group. Zweitens: Group game matters; include friends before isolating. Drittens: Kiss can happen in venue or outside. Viertens: Hotel should be central and known before the night. Fünftens: Do not let a second club become an unnecessary detour.

Duesseldorf. Erstens: Compact geography rewards short walks and fast moves. Zweitens: Fashion/status observations fit naturally. Drittens: High chance of overlapping social circles: remain discreet. Viertens: Bar-to-taxi-to-hotel is cleaner than a long dancefloor phase. Fünftens: Direct plan beats “what do you want to do?”.

Abschnitt: Travel-city route.
