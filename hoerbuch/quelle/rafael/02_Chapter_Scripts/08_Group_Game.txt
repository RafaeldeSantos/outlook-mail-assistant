Kapitel 7. Group Game.

Create one natural shared moment, then focus gradually. Friend management is social intelligence, not obstacle removal.

Abschnitt: Group sequence.

Phase: Open all. Aktion: Situational observation or light role question. Avoid: Immediately locking only onto the target. Phase: Shared moment. Aktion: One short riff that lets everyone contribute. Avoid: Performing a five-minute canned routine. Phase: Read the room. Aktion: Who invests, who protects the group, who wants to leave?. Avoid: Assuming politeness equals attraction. Phase: Target focus. Aktion: More eye contact, one specific follow-up, one callback. Avoid: Ignoring the friend. Phase: Friend bridge. Aktion: Name, small value, transparent mini-plan. Avoid: Framing her as blocker. Phase: Micro-isolate. Aktion: “Ich klaue sie euch fünf Minuten für frische Luft.”. Avoid: Disappearing without notice.

Abschnitt: Natural group lines.

High-energy only. „Welche ist die Wildeste von euch?“ kann in einem lauten, spielerischen Club funktionieren. In einer Premium-Lounge ist es keine Standard-Line.
