Kapitel 27, Teil 2. English Field Scripts, Teil 2.

Sprich die englischen Sätze langsam, klar und ohne komplizierte Wörter. Die Wirkung kommt aus Ruhe, Blickkontakt und einem einfachen Plan.

English field script 9. English: Group leaving.

Open.

“Are you leaving already? I was just about to meet you.”

Run the interaction.

Ask destination; two-minute interaction; suggest one drink only if practical.

Transition / close.

“Come for one quick drink before you go.”

English field script 10. English: Friend blocker.

Open.

“Tell your friends where we are going. We are only outside for five minutes.”

Run the interaction.

Be transparent; do not argue with friend.

Transition / close.

Return or update as promised.

English field script 11. English: Kiss window.

Open.

“I would like to kiss you.”

Run the interaction.

Pause; let her answer and move.

Transition / close.

“No problem. We can slow down.”

English field script 12. English: Hotel transition.

Open.

“I can come up for one glass of water if you want. Ten minutes, relaxed.”

Run the interaction.

Direct and optional first. Only after a kiss or very clear mutual sexual chemistry add the playful role reversal.

Transition / close.

“One glass of water. Ten minutes. And behave.”

If she hesitates: “No problem. Good night.”

English field script 13. English: She is too drunk.

Open.

“We are not doing anything tonight. Let us get you back to your friends.”

Run the interaction.

Water, friends, taxi, hotel staff.

Transition / close.

No sexual escalation.

English field script 14. English: Short answers.

Open.

“You are very efficient with words tonight.”

Run the interaction.

One genuine follow-up to her last real detail or one observation. If the energy stays low, exit.

Transition / close.

“No worries. Have a good night.”

English field script 15. English: Strong investment.

Open.

“You are making it very easy to like you.”

Run the interaction.

Warmth back; position change; kiss window.

Transition / close.

Do not manufacture coldness.

English field script 16. English: Reconnect in Marbella.

Open.

“I am back in Marbella next Friday. One drink at nine?”

Run the interaction.

Low frequency, high clarity. No artificial silence and no weeks of background chat.

Transition / close.

If the response is vague twice, stop carrying the conversation.
