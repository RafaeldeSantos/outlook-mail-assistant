Kapitel 5. Selectivity Without Inaction.

Abundance is useful only if it does not become a reason to avoid the one woman you actually want.

Your selection rule. Erstens: Warm-up sets: maximum two minutes, no forced escalation. Zweitens: Interesting: five-minute evaluation, only continue if energy and visual attraction rise. Drittens: Top: full focus, fast progression, no background scanning.

Anti-rationalization. Erstens: “She is too surrounded” is not a reason; it is a group-game task. Zweitens: “I will approach later” usually means never. Drittens: “The line is not good enough” means use a simpler line. Viertens: “I need more state” after 45 minutes means approach now.

Abschnitt: Two-minute warm-up exit.

Beispiel: “Ich hole mir kurz etwas zu trinken. War nett, euch kennenzulernen.”

No apology, no number collection, no fake investment. The purpose is voice, timing and social calibration.
