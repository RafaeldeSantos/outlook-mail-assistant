Kapitel 21. 90-Day Training System.

Thirteen weeks to preserve the strong core and automate the evidence-filtered upgrades.

Week: 1. Focus: State and voice. Mission: 10 micro-interactions, 5 passing compliments, 3 real sets per night.. Week: 2. Focus: Solo movement. Mission: 45 minutes independent movement; friends remain base.. Week: 3. Focus: Natural group opening. Mission: Five group opens; no canned role routine longer than one exchange.. Week: 4. Focus: BISS and follow-up. Mission: Observation – interpretation – play – step; one real follow-up in every hooked set.. Week: 5. Focus: Callbacks. Mission: Ten callbacks built from details actually given by the woman.. Week: 6. Focus: Warm teasing. Mission: No negs; specific compliment, warm tease and reward after investment.. Week: 7. Focus: Position changes. Mission: Five accepted small moves and the signals that justified them.. Week: 8. Focus: Reciprocity gates. Mission: Name two current green signals before isolation, kiss or private transition.. Week: 9. Focus: Frankfurt two-route system. Mission: Hotel-guest route and local route; direct invite or concrete Tier-2 close.. Week: 10. Focus: Friend management. Mission: One natural shared moment before target focus; transparent mini-plan.. Week: 11. Focus: Simple English. Mission: Thirty short interactions; follow-up questions and simple transitions.. Week: 12. Focus: Full funnel. Mission: Three serious sets progressed as far as mutual conditions allowed.. Week: 13. Focus: 15% purge audit. Mission: Remove one scripted, cold or unnecessary behavior; keep what measurably converts..

Abschnitt: Weekly minimum.
